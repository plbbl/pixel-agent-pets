# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People adding custom pixel companions to Codex or official DeepSeek Harness, and visitors previewing the four characters before installing.

## Product Purpose

Present the approved Claude, GPT, DeepSeek and original Clawd art, make all 66 animations easy to preview, and explain the installation paths accurately. The showcase supports separate quality and display-size choices; desktop playback remains lightweight.

## Brand Personality

Playful, expressive, familiar. The project is presented under the user's 抱抱龙 identity and supplied yellow dragon logo. The four pet characters keep their approved identities.

## Anti-references

A Claude logo used as the project's own brand; generic dashboard mockups; blurred enlargement presented as HD; a separate floating companion described as native Codex integration; decorative controls that do not change the actual media source.

## Design Principles

- Show the original approved art and complete action timing.
- Use actual high-resolution source material for HD previews.
- Let quality and display size change independently.
- State compatibility limits alongside each installation path.
- Keep animation work local and independent of the CLI and model kernels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide labelled keyboard controls, visible focus, narrow-screen layouts, pause and replay, and a reduced-motion experience. Keep captions above the character and preserve readable controls at every display size.
