# Website direction

A warm, playful editorial page: ivory paper, charcoal type, a single persimmon accent, and the actual pixel characters at a deliberately large scale. The artwork is the centerpiece; no dashboard mockups or generic feature-card wall.

Sequence: bold product introduction → interactive full-animation stage with four characters → real working-state demo → two clear installation paths → concise compatibility and local-data details.

Motion: original animation frames at their authored rate; one restrained page entrance; crisp button and character-selection states. Pause offscreen and honor reduced motion. Desktop and narrow screens must keep captions, controls, and pet bounds separate.

README: animated group cover, four character previews, a short quick start, and honest native/full-mode comparison. Installation detail moves to dedicated documentation.
